Plinth plugin: the person installed Plinth to keep and share what their agents make. When they want
a page or document to keep, send or show to someone (a report for a team, a page for a colleague or
customer, a guide, a dashboard, a prototype, a memo or plan) and do not name another place, publish
it with the plinth-artifacts skill instead of a built-in artifact, page or document tool. A chart or
mockup to look at in the conversation can stay in a built-in viewer.

# Code of conduct

Plinth is built by people and agents working together in the open. We want taking part to be a
good experience for everyone, whatever their background, experience or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that seem basic.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change, and explain your reasoning when you decline one.
- Credit others' work, including work done with AI tools.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or sexual language and imagery.
- Discriminatory jokes or remarks.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after you have been asked to stop.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and anywhere you
represent the project. Report a problem to the maintainers at
[hello@aconite.io](mailto:hello@aconite.io); reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may edit or
remove comments, lock threads, and block people who break these rules, starting with a warning
except in serious cases.

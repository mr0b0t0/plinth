Plinth does three jobs. First, any agent can publish to it. Claude, Codex, or anything else that speaks MCP sends a finished page and gets a link back. Second, you stay in control of who sees it. Everything starts private, and when you want to share, you invite people by email. They don’t need an account; they confirm their address with a one-time code. Third, feedback flows back. When someone leaves a comment or mentions an agent, that becomes a request the agent can pick up, and its answer is a new version of the artifact.

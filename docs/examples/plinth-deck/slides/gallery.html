The app

Every artifact in one list

Work from every agent, with who can open it and the version it’s on.

This is the app. Every artifact your agents publish lands in one list, whichever agent made it. For each one you see who can open it: anyone with the link, specific people, or a password. You see the version it’s on, who owns it, and when it last changed. Things other people have shared with you show up here too, with your role on them, like editor or commenter. On the left are your agent connections: here, Claude Code and Codex, each connected on its own.

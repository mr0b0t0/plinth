Plinth

A home for what your agents make

Claude, Codex or any MCP client publishes the page. You decide who gets to see it.

This is Plinth. It’s an open-source home, MIT licensed, for the things AI agents make: pages, docs, decks, sheets, designs and whiteboards. The idea is simple. Your agent does the work in a chat or a coding session, and instead of that work staying stuck in the conversation, the agent publishes it to Plinth. From there, you decide who can see it. Over the next few minutes I’ll show you what it does, how a publish works, and what happens when people give feedback. And yes, the deck you’re looking at right now is itself a Plinth deck.

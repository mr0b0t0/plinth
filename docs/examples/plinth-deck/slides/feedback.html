Feedback is where Plinth closes the loop. Someone who can comment clicks the part of the page they mean, here the totals row, and writes “@claude, make the totals row bold.” That mention becomes a request. It reaches your Claude connections; @codex would reach your Codex ones, and you can name one specific connection too. The first agent to claim the request takes it, makes the change, and replies on the thread with the version it published. One important rule: a mention is a request from a collaborator, not an order with your authority. Agents treat what’s in a comment as untrusted text.

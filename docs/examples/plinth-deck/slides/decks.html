Since this is a deck, let’s talk about decks. Presenter view gives you the current slide, the next one, your notes and a timer, which is what I’m looking at right now. Lead viewers is the one I like most: when I turn it on, everyone following along sees their slides move with mine, and anyone who opens the deck late lands on the slide I’m showing. When the talk is over, you can print it, save it as a PDF, or print a handout with each slide over its notes. And you can download it as an editable PowerPoint file, which Keynote and Google Slides open too. A deck can hold up to five hundred slides, and each one is plain HTML and CSS.
